<h2>🔧 How to Use the Mod Manager</h2>
<h3>First Launch</h3>
<p>When you open the app for the first time, you'll see a main window with a list of mods. You can browse all available mods in the Workshop tab. To install a mod, just click it and hit the "Install" button. The manager handles the rest.</p>

<h3>Managing Your Mods</h3>
<p>In the "My Mods" tab, you'll see all the mods you have installed. You can enable or disable them with a toggle switch. If a mod has a dependency problem, a warning icon will appear. Click on it to see details. For conflicts, the app will highlight both conflicting mods in red.</p>

<h3>Creating and Switching Profiles</h3>
<p>Click on the "Profiles" button at the top. Here you can create a new profile (like "Survival Playthrough" or "Creative Build"). Name it and then select which mods you want in that profile. To switch profiles, just click on the profile name and choose "Activate". The mods change immediately.</p>

<h3>Updating Mods</h3>
<p>Check for updates by clicking the "Check for Updates" button. The app will scan all your installed mods. If any need updating, a list appears. Click "Update All" to apply all updates at once.</p>
